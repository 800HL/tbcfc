Questions

New to digital assets? Start here.

What is a stablecoin?

A digital dollar. Each USDC or USDT is designed to always be worth one US dollar, so the value doesn’t jump around like Bitcoin. It’s the simplest way to give across borders.

What app do I need?

Any app that holds digital assets and can send them, such as Coinbase, Binance, Kraken, Revolut, Luno, VALR, MetaMask or Trust Wallet. If your app shows a “Send” or “Withdraw” button, you’re ready.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

Usually a few minutes. You pay a small network fee, often cents on the networks marked “Lowest fees”. Your app shows the exact fee before you confirm.

Is it safe? Where does my gift go?

Your gift goes to The Bay’s account at a South African exchange licensed by the Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA). The church converts every gift to rand the same day, so its value is locked in.

I sent the wrong coin or network. What now?

Email giving@thebaycfc.org with your transaction ID straight away. Some mistakes can be fixed by the exchange, but not all, which is why it’s worth checking before you send.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. Fill in the form above and our giving team will acknowledge your gift. Tax rules differ by country, so please check with your own adviser about any deductions.
