Questions

What is a stablecoin?

A digital dollar. 1 USDC or 1 USDT is worth US$1.

Which app can I use?

Any app that can send crypto, such as Coinbase, Binance, Revolut, Luno, VALR or MetaMask.

What does it cost?

A small network fee, shown in your app before you send.

Where does my gift go?

To the church’s account at an FSCA-licensed South African exchange. It is converted to rand the same day.
